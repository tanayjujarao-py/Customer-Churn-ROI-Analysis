## **QUESTION 1: What's our actual Churn Situation?**

## 

## **Overall Churn Metrics**

* **Total Customers:** 8,000  
* **Churned Customers:** 3,073  
* **Churn Rate:** 38.4%  
* **Industry Benchmark:** 25-30%  
* **Status:** Significantly above benchmark

**Churn by Subscription Tier**

* **Basic:** 46.8% Churn Rate, 1879 customers Churned  
* **Plus:** 32.4% Churn Rate, 923 customers Churned  
* **Premium:** 23.9% Churn Rate, 271 customers Churned

**Churn by Tenure**

* **0-3 months:** 59.3% Churn Rate (**← CRITICAL PERIOD**)  
* **4-6 months:** 51.9% Churn Rate  
* **7-12 months:** 39.1% Churn Rate  
* **12+ months:** 27.4% Churn Rate

**Churn by Acquisition Channel**

* **Paid Social:** 49.5% Churn Rate, 85$ Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)  
* **Paid Search:** 41.8% Churn Rate, 95$ CAC  
* **Email:** 35.4% Churn Rate, 15$ CAC  
* **Referral:** 34.3% Churn Rate, 25$ CAC  
* **Organic:** 34.4% Churn Rate, 45$ CAC  
* **Direct:** 35.1% Churn Rate, 0$ CAC

**Churn by Billing Cycle**

* **Monthly:** 43.5% Churn Rate  
* **Annual:** 26.4% Churn Rate

\-----**Segment Performance Summary**

* **WORST Segment:** Basic \+ Monthly \+ Paid Social/Pair Search \+ First 6 months (75%+ Churn Rate)  
* **BEST Segment:** Premium \+ Annual \+ Organic Search \+ More than 12 months (3% Churn Rate)

**Key Insight**

## The churn problem is concentrated in **Basic Tier** customers in their first 6 months, especially those acquired via **Paid Search or Paid Social** on **Monthly Billing cycle**. If customers survive the first 6 months, retention improves dramatically.
