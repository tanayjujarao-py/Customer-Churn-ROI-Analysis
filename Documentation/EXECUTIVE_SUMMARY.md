# **Executive Summary: Strategic Churn Mitigation & ROI Analysis**

**To:** Board of Directors / Executive Leadership

**From:** Tanay Ravindra Jujarao, Data Analyst | Business Intelligence

**Date:** March 19, 2026

**Subject:** Retention Strategy to Recover $853K in Annualized Revenue

---

## **1\. The Situation**

Customer churn has reached 38.4% (vs. 25–30% benchmark), resulting in a $3.2M annual revenue loss . This crisis is driven primarily by support inefficiencies and failed early customer activation. Analysis of 8,000 customers and 16.6K interactions confirms this is an operational issue, not a product-market fit failure.

## **2\. Strategic Insights**

* **Critical Support Friction:** Customers with 6+ support tickets churn at **97.6%**.  
* **Resolution Speed Gap:** Slow response times (48hr+) drive an **82.1% churn rate**, a 4.3x increase over fast resolution.  
* **The 6-Month Cliff:** 59.3% of churn occurs within the first 90 days. Retention stabilizes significantly (27.4%) for customers reaching the 12-month mark.  
* **Activation Failure:** Customers who never place an order churn at **67.1%**. Early activation within 14 days is the strongest predictor of long-term loyalty.

## **3\. Targeted Recommendations & Investment**

A total investment of **$270,000** is required to stabilize the revenue base.

Priority 1: Support Crisis Intervention ($165K)

* **Action:** Hire 3 specialized agents ($135K) and upgrade ticketing infrastructure ($30K).  
* **Goal:** Maintain sub-24hr resolution and implement priority flagging for high-risk segments.  
* **Logic:** Prioritized due to direct impact on 67% of total churn revenue loss.

Priority 2: Activation & Onboarding Program ($105K)

* **Action:** Deploy automated email/SMS sequences and a 20% first-order incentive.  
* **Goal:** Drive first-purchase completion within the 14-day "Golden Window".

## **4\. Financial Impact & ROI**

* **Annual Revenue Recovered:** $852,929.  
* **Return on Investment (ROI):** **3.16x** in Year 1\.  
* **Payback Period:** **3.8 Months**.  
* **Note:** Estimates based on conservative scenario modeling.
