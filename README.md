## 💡 Key Business Insights
* [cite_start]**The Support "Death Spiral":** Customers with 6+ support tickets churn at **97.6%**, indicating severe friction in the resolution process[cite: 1].
* [cite_start]**Resolution Speed Impact:** Slow response times (48hr+) drive an **82.1% churn rate**, a 4.3x increase over fast resolution (<24hr)[cite: 1].
* [cite_start]**The 0-6 Month Risk Window:** **59.3% of churn** occurs within the first 90 days of the customer lifecycle[cite: 1].
* [cite_start]**Activation Failure:** Customers who never place an order churn at **67.1%**; early activation within 14 days is the strongest predictor of long-term loyalty[cite: 1].
